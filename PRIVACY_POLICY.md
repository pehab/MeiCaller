# Privacy Policy – MeiCaller

MeiCaller is an Android phone and dialer application.

## Data processing

MeiCaller reads contacts, call history and phone/call state on the device to provide its dialer, favorites, call-log and in-call functions. This phone and contact content is processed locally by MeiCaller and is not uploaded to a MeiCaller server.

The app does not contain advertising and does not use Firebase Analytics.

## Crash reporting

MeiCaller uses **Firebase Crashlytics** to diagnose crashes and improve app stability. Crash reports can contain technical information such as the app version, device and operating-system information, stack traces, app state and diagnostic installation identifiers. Google/Firebase processes this technical data to provide Crashlytics.

Crashlytics is not used to upload contacts, call-log entries or call contents.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/

## Permissions

MeiCaller uses Android permissions required for its core telephone functionality, including:

- contacts, to display contact names, photos and favorites;
- call log, to display incoming, outgoing and missed calls;
- phone state, to react to call state and provide the in-call UI;
- call phone, to initiate calls;
- vibration and wake-lock functionality where required by the call UI.

## Local data

App preferences such as visual settings are stored locally on the device. Local app data can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling MeiCaller.

## Data sharing

Personal data is not sold. Contact and call-log content is not shared by MeiCaller. Technical crash information is processed by Google/Firebase only for the Crashlytics functionality described above.

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The current version is published in this repository.
