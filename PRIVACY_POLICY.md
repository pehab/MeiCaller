# Privacy Policy – MeiCaller

MeiCaller is a local phone and dialer application for Android.

## Data collection
MeiCaller does **not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data**.
All data (contacts, call log, phone state) is accessed **locally on the device only**.

## Permissions
MeiCaller requires certain Android permissions to provide its core phone functionality:

- Contacts: to display contact names, photos, and favorites
- Call log: to show incoming, outgoing, and missed calls
- Phone state: to detect call status and display the in-call screen
- Call phone: to place phone calls

This data is **never sent off the device** and is **not stored permanently** by the app.

## Third parties
MeiCaller does not use analytics, tracking, ads, or third-party SDKs.
